Community Engagement Project (CEP)

About Smart Krishi Sahayak

A multilingual, precision agricultural intelligence system bridging scientific agronomy and grassroots farming through modern technology and Artificial Intelligence.

Our Mission

Empowering Indian Farmers with Real-Time Agronomic Knowledge

Agriculture is the backbone of India, engaging over 50% of the national workforce. However, smallholder and marginal farmers often face severe yield losses due to untimely pest attacks, erratic weather patterns, lack of localized soil awareness, and linguistic barriers in accessing modern agricultural literature.

Smart Krishi Sahayak was created as a collegiate Community Engagement Project by Computer Science & Engineering students to deliver accessible, actionable, and scientific precision farming advice in native Indian languages (मराठी, हिंदी, English).

Native Multilingual

Seamless switching across Marathi, Hindi, and English so every farmer understands instructions without barriers.

AI Agronomist

Context-aware chatbot powered by LLM and domain-grounded knowledge for crop, pest, and soil management.

Weather & Climate

Hyper-local meteorological alerts and stage-specific advisories to protect against frost, rain, and heat stress.

Disease Diagnostic

Image-based plant disease detection offering both organic remedies and chemical integrated pest management.

Modern Architecture

Production-Grade Technology Stack

Designed using a decoupled RESTful architecture ready to power web and future Flutter mobile apps without structural re-engineering.
